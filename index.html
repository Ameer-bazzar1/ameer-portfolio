Featured product

DORAK

Mobile group-savings app · Qatar

DORAK is a digital savings-circle app (like a jam‘iyya). I build and maintain it. Members join groups, pay on a schedule, and get updates on their phone. Admins review payments and run the groups from the same product.

  • iOS and Android app with Flutter, secure login, and fingerprint/face unlock.
  • Django REST backend, PostgreSQL, Celery background jobs, and live updates.
  • Push notifications. The whole app is available in English and Arabic.
  • Group management, payments, ID checks, two-factor login, and admin dashboards.
  • Deployed with Docker on Railway; extra device-security checks on the phone.
  • Flutter
  • Django
  • PostgreSQL
  • Celery
  • Firebase
